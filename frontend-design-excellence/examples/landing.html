Why onboarding drop-off spikes on day 3

Edited 2h ago · 4 sources

Across the twelve interviews, the pattern was consistent: users who hadn’t invited a teammate by day 3 rarely came back, regardless of how much they’d set up alone.

This contradicts our assumption that solo setup depth predicts retention. The Q3 cohort data points the same way.
