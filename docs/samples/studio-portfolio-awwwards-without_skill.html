(01) — Intro

We're Northlight. Three people in a converted boathouse on the Oslo fjord, making identities that move for festivals that run all night and brands that live outside. We like weather, volume and long winters — and work that looks just as good on a 40-metre LED wall as on a sticker.

Founded 2019 by Ingrid Solberg (design), Mats Aalvik (motion) and Sunniva Haug (strategy & production). No account managers — you talk to the people doing the work.

Small on purpose. We take on six to eight projects a year, so each one gets our full attention from first sketch to the last frame.
