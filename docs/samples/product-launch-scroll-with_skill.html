Introducing Aero

Lift the lid. Leave the noise.

01  Adaptive noise cancelling

The world, turned down to a line.

Microphones on each bud read the room continuously and cancel what doesn’t belong — the train, the fan, the open-plan office — while your music stays exactly as it was mixed.

02  Battery

40h

Bring the case. Forget the charger.

Up to 8 hours in the buds and four more full charges in the case — 40 hours in total. Ten minutes on USB‑C adds two more.

03  Fit

4.6 grams. Gone the moment they’re in.

A low, rounded shell that sits flush in the ear instead of out of it, with three silicone tip sizes in the box. Tap the face to play, pause or switch modes.
