The studio

Northlight is three people in an old print shop on Grünerløkka. We make identities for festivals that run past midnight and outdoor brands that live in bad weather: systems built to move, from a 40-metre stage screen down to a 16-pixel app icon.

What we do

Four disciplines, one small team

01

Brand identity

Names, marks, type and colour systems that hold up everywhere a festival or brand shows up: from a wristband to a 40-metre stage.

  • Strategy workshop
  • Logo & wordmark
  • Type & colour
  • Guidelines
02

Motion identity

Logos that move, animated type, idents and the visuals that play behind the headliner. Designed in motion from the first sketch, not animated afterwards.

  • Motion principles
  • Logo animation
  • LED & stage
  • Social toolkits
03

Festival systems

Everything a festival needs to look like itself for three days: posters, line-up layouts, wayfinding in the dark, merch and the app.

  • Poster & line-up
  • Wayfinding
  • Merch
  • Ticketing UI
04

Campaign & film

Art direction and title design for launches and seasonal campaigns, shot in the places the product is actually made for.

  • Art direction
  • Title design
  • Launch film
  • Out of home
