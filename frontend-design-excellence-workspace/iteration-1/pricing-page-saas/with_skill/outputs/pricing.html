Questions

Can't find what you need? Email billing@taskflow.app and a person will reply within a business day.

Who counts as a paid user?

Anyone on your team who can create and edit projects. Freelance guests on Studio and Agency, and clients who only use the client portal, are free and don't count toward your total.

How does "2 months free" work on annual billing?

Pay for a year up front and you're charged for 10 months instead of 12. Studio comes to $120 per user per year instead of $144, and Agency to $240 instead of $288.

What happens when Starter hits 3 projects?

Nothing gets deleted. You can archive a finished project to free up a slot, or upgrade to Studio for unlimited projects. Archived projects stay searchable and can be restored at any time.

What happens if we add people mid-year?

New members are billed pro rata for the rest of your billing period. If someone leaves, remove them and the seat is credited to your next invoice.

Can we switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we credit what's left of your current plan. Downgrades apply at the end of your billing period, and Agency-only data such as timesheets stays exportable.

What do clients see in a client portal?

Only what you share: selected projects, milestones, files, and items waiting on their approval. Portals use your logo and colors, and internal comments and time entries are never visible to clients.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of Studio or Agency with no card required. When the trial ends you choose a plan, or your workspace moves to Starter and keeps your three most recent projects active.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Registered nonprofits and education teams get 40% off any paid plan. Email us from your organization's domain to apply.
