Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email our team and we'll reply within one business day.

How does per-user pricing work?

You pay for each person on your team who has a TaskFlow account. Clients who log in to a client portal and guests you invite to a project are free and don't count as users.

How does annual billing get me 2 months free?

If you pay for a year up front, you're charged for 10 months instead of 12. That works out to $10 per user per month on Studio (instead of $12) and $20 on Agency (instead of $24). You can switch from monthly to annual billing at any time.

What happens when I go past 3 projects on Starter?

Your existing projects keep working. To create a fourth active project, you can archive an old one or upgrade to Studio. Archived projects stay searchable and don't count toward the limit.

Can I add or remove people during my billing period?

Yes. When you add someone, we charge a prorated amount for the rest of your billing period. When you remove someone, we add a credit to your next invoice.

What are client portals?

On the Agency plan, each client gets their own branded space with your logo and colors. There, they can follow project progress, approve deliverables and leave feedback, without seeing your internal tasks or conversations.

Does the free trial need a credit card?

No. Studio and Agency both come with a 14-day trial with every feature unlocked. When the trial ends, you choose a plan or your workspace moves to Starter. Your data is kept either way.

Can I change plans or cancel later?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your workspace settings at any time. If you cancel an annual plan, you keep access until the end of the paid year.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and schools get 30% off any paid plan. Contact us and we'll set it up.
